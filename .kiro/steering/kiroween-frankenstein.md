# Kiroween Frankenstein narrative

Interview Fit combines distinct systems into one coherent desktop product:

- Tauri/Rust as the local security boundary.
- React/TypeScript for the guided desktop experience.
- Webcam or photo capture with privacy-aware preprocessing.
- YouCam photo-quality and skin-prep analysis and one hairstyle try-on through a secure backend proxy.
- Claude (Anthropic) job and company-atmosphere analysis and interview-style recommendations, policy-checked with a deterministic rules fallback.
- Kiro specs, steering, prompts, and hooks as the engineering harness.

When documenting Kiro usage, record concrete decisions and corrections rather than generic statements. Include which requirement, design decision, task, hook, or review prevented a defect or changed the product.
