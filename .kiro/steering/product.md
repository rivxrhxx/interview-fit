# Product

Interview Fit helps a candidate prepare their visual presentation for an interview without judging their appearance or predicting hiring outcomes.

## Primary user

An adult candidate preparing for an online or in-person interview who wants practical, optional guidance tied to a job description.

## MVP journey

1. Enter a job description.
2. Review the job and company-atmosphere analysis (role, industry, workplace atmosphere, interview format, dress-code context), each point backed by a quote from the posting.
3. Upload a selfie or use the camera.
4. Preview and confirm the image.
5. Read and affirm just-in-time consent.
6. Run YouCam photo-quality and skin-prep analysis through the first-party backend.
7. Review neutral observations and AI interview-style suggestions tied to the job analysis.
8. Try one curated interview hairstyle on (YouCam hairstyle try-on).
9. Compare before and after, then export a privacy-aware preparation report.

## Success criteria

- A first-time user completes the golden path without instructions.
- The demo shows one complete job analysis, photo analysis, and hairstyle try-on flow.
- No provider secret is present in any desktop artifact.
- Cancellation and failure paths clean up temporary image data.
- Recommendations explain why they relate to job-context cues.

## Non-goals

- Medical or dermatological diagnosis. Skin analysis is used only to produce optional lighting and grooming-prep tips; no scores, skin age, skin type, or condition names are shown.
- Attractiveness, beauty, skin, or facial-proportion scores.
- Personality, emotion, competence, honesty, or employability inference.
- Inferring gender or other traits to pick styles; the user chooses the hairstyle.
- Candidate ranking or hiring recommendations.
- Identity recognition or face matching.
- Accounts, history, social sharing, open-ended catalogs, and more than one try-on category in the MVP.
